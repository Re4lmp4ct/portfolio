Let's Gem originally started out as an adventure game about diving deep into the mines to harvest gems, it focused more
on the platforming and exploration aspect. However, during playtesting, we received feedback that while breaking the gems
was the most fun aspect, it was too rare of an experience. As we updated the game direction to move towards an arcade,
where the focus was smashing gems.
