Singapore Polytechnic collaborated with The South West Community Development Council (CDC) to create games that promotes 
having a positive mindset for grassroots engagement. Our team developed Dream Dash with the goal of promoting a positive 
outlook towards exercise. We were the only team whose game got selected by the council to showcase at their main
grassroot event.
